Privacy Policy

Sylvia's Budget Tool (the "app")
Published by Sylvia's Apps
Last updated: 11/23/2026

The short version

Your budget data stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no servers and no analytics, and I never receive your financial information.

Something leaves your device only when you choose it: tapping the optional "Watch an ad" button, which uses Google's ad service (section 4), or sending me a bug report, which you see in full first and which contains no amounts or names (section 5).

1. Information stored on your device

The app lets you enter information such as your income (and, if you budget as a household, the names and incomes of the people in it), budget categories, spending and bill entries with descriptions and due dates, savings and retirement accounts, savings goals, the value of your home, car or other property, debts, credit scores and credit cards, estate-planning and insurance checklist answers, and your monthly history. All of this is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. It is not uploaded, synced or shared with me or anyone else.

I do not run servers for this app, I do not require an account, and I do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts or any other personal information.

2. Backups, exports and imports

The app can create backup and export files (JSON backups, CSV spreadsheets and PDF reports). These are saved to a location you choose using Android's file picker. Once a file is saved, it is yours: the app does not track where it goes. You are responsible for storing and sharing exported files safely, since they may contain sensitive financial details. Importing a file works the same way, and the app only reads the file you select.

Android may back up the app's data to your Google account as part of your device's own backup feature. That backup is controlled by your device and Google account settings, and I do not have access to it. The app's diagnostic log (section 5) is excluded from these backups.

3. What I do not do

I do not sell your personal information. I do not use analytics or tracking tools, and the app never sends crash or usage reports on its own. I do not build profiles of users.

4. The optional "Watch an ad" button

The About screen has an optional button that lets you support the app by watching a short ad. Ads appear nowhere else in the app.

  • Nothing runs until you choose it. The ad service is not started when the app opens. It starts only after you tap the button. If you never tap it, no ad code runs and no ad-related data leaves your device.
  • Who provides it. Ads are provided by Google LLC through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Once started, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use information from your device, such as your IP address, device and app information, ad interactions, diagnostic information and, if you have not opted out or deleted it, your Android advertising ID. They use it to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and keep the service secure.
  • Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), Google's consent form is shown before an ad loads, and ads are personalized only if you agree. You can change that choice later with "Ad privacy choices" on the About screen, which appears once the form has been shown. Elsewhere, whether ads are personalized depends on your device's ad settings.
  • What I receive. I do not receive this device-level data. I see only aggregate reports from AdMob (for example, the number of ads shown and revenue), which do not identify you.
  • Your budget data is never shared. The app does not send any budget, account, transaction or goal information to Google or any advertiser.
  • Your controls. You can skip the button entirely with no loss of any feature. You can delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device's Android settings (usually under Privacy → Ads).

To learn how Google handles this data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Diagnostic log and bug reports

To help fix problems, the app keeps a small diagnostic log on your device. It records what the app did, such as which screens were opened, actions like adding or deleting an entry (identified by category, not by what you typed), settings you changed, reminders that ran, errors and crash details, plus the app version, your device model and Android version. It does not record amounts, balances, names or descriptions you type. Only the most recent lines are kept, it stays in the app's private storage, it is excluded from Android backups, and "Reset app" clears it.

The log is never sent automatically. You can send it to me only by choosing "Report a bug" on the About screen (the app also offers this after an unexpected close). Before anything is sent:

  • The report is shown to you in full, and the send buttons unlock only after you have scrolled to the end of it.
  • Names you have typed (such as custom categories, accounts, debts, goals and people) are replaced with placeholders, and amounts, email addresses, phone numbers and long numbers are removed.
  • The report includes a summary of how the app is set up (counts and on/off settings only, never amounts) and anything you write in the "What happened?" box.

"Send by email" opens your own email app with the report filled in, and nothing is sent until you send that email. "Copy report" and "Save app log" in Settings put the same report on your clipboard or in a file you choose. Reports you email me are used only to investigate and fix problems, are not shared with anyone else, and are deleted once they are no longer needed. You can ask me to delete a report at any time.

6. Support and community links (Ko-fi, Patreon, Discord)

The About screen includes links to support the developer on Ko-fi and Patreon and to join a Discord community. These links open in your web browser or the matching app. They are run by independent third parties, and the app does not share any information with them.

When you follow a link you leave the app, and that service's own privacy policy and terms apply. They may collect information such as your IP address and any account details you give them. All payments are handled by those services. I never see or store your card or bank details, and the app does not process payments. Support is voluntary and does not unlock anything in the app.

7. Permissions the app may request

  • Notifications (optional): used only for reminders you turn on: a daily spending reminder, a notice when a new budget month starts, and alerts when a bill you entered is due soon or overdue. They are scheduled and worked out on your device. There is no push-notification server.
  • Biometric or PIN lock (optional): handled by Android. The app never receives or stores your fingerprint, face or PIN data.
  • Files: import, export, PDF reports and saving the app log use Android's file picker, so the app can only access files you choose.
  • Network access and advertising ID: used only by Google's ad service for the optional ad feature described in section 4. Bug reports are sent through your own email app, not by the app itself.

8. Children

The app is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed to children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Security and deleting your data

Your data is kept in the app's private storage on your device and is protected by Android's app sandbox and your device lock. You can turn on the app's optional lock for extra protection. Because your data lives only on your device, you can delete all of it at any time with "Reset app" in Settings, by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling the app. If you lose or reset your phone without a backup file, I cannot recover your data, because I do not have a copy. Making regular backups is recommended.

10. Your rights and choices

Because I do not collect or hold your personal information, there is nothing for me to access, correct or delete on my side, apart from any bug report or email you choose to send me, which I will delete on request. For data collected by Google when you choose to watch an ad, your rights are described in Google's privacy policy, and you can contact Google directly. If you live somewhere with privacy rights, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California, you can still email me with a question or request.

11. Changes to this policy

If I change how the app handles data, I will update this page and the "Last updated" date, and mention significant changes in the app's release notes.
