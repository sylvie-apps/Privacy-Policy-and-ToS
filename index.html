Privacy Policy

Sylvia's Budget Tool (the "app")
Published by Sylvia's Apps
Last updated: 11/21/2026

The short version

Your budget data stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no servers and no analytics, and I never receive your financial information.

The only time anything leaves your device is if you choose to tap the optional "watch an ad" button, which uses Google's ad service. Details are in section 4.

1. Information stored on your device

The app lets you enter budget categories, expenses, income, savings goals, retirement account details, debts and related notes. All of this is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. It is not uploaded, synced or shared with me or anyone else.

I do not run servers for this app, I do not require an account, and I do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts or any other personal information.

2. Backups, exports and imports

The app can create backup and export files (such as JSON, CSV or PDF). These are saved to a location you choose using Android's file picker. Once a file is saved, it is yours: the app does not track where it goes. You are responsible for storing and sharing exported files safely, since they may contain sensitive financial details. Importing a file works the same way, and the app only reads the file you select.

Android may back up the app's data to your Google account as part of your device's own backup feature. That backup is controlled by your device and Google account settings, and I do not have access to it.

3. What I do not do

I do not sell your personal information. I do not use analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools of my own. I do not build profiles of users.

4. The optional "watch an ad" button

The About screen has an optional button that lets you support the app by watching a short ad. Ads appear nowhere else in the app.

  • Nothing runs until you choose it. The ad service is not started when the app opens. It starts only after you tap the button and agree to the prompt the app shows first. If you never tap the button, no ad code runs and no ad-related data leaves your device.
  • Who provides it. Ads are provided by Google LLC through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Once started, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use information from your device, such as your IP address, device and app information, ad interactions, diagnostic information and, if you have not opted out or reset it, your Android advertising ID. They use it to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and keep the service secure.
  • What I receive. I do not receive this device-level data. I see only aggregate reports from AdMob (for example, the number of ads shown and revenue), which do not identify you.
  • Your budget data is never shared. The app does not send any budget, account, transaction or goal information to Google or any advertiser.
  • Personalization and consent. Ads are requested as non-personalized. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or certain US states), you will also be shown Google's consent form before an ad loads.
  • Your controls. You can skip the button entirely with no loss of any feature. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device's Android settings (usually under Privacy → Ads).

To learn how Google handles this data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Support and community links (Ko-fi, Patreon, Discord)

The About screen includes links to support the developer on Ko-fi and Patreon and to join a Discord community. These links open in your web browser or the matching app. They are run by independent third parties, and the app does not share any information with them.

When you follow a link you leave the app, and that service's own privacy policy and terms apply. They may collect information such as your IP address and any account details you give them. All payments are handled by those services. I never see or store your card or bank details, and the app does not process payments. Support is voluntary and does not unlock anything in the app.

6. Permissions the app may request

  • Notifications (optional): used only for reminders you turn on. They are scheduled on your device. There is no push-notification server.
  • Biometric or PIN lock (optional): handled by Android. The app never receives or stores your fingerprint or face data.
  • Files: import and export use Android's file picker, so the app can only access files you choose.
  • Network access: used only for the optional ad feature described in section 4.

7. Children

The app is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed to children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security and deleting your data

Your data is kept in the app's private storage on your device and is protected by Android's app sandbox and your device lock. You can add the app's optional lock for extra protection. Because your data lives only on your device, you can delete all of it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. If you lose or reset your phone without a backup file, I cannot recover your data, because I do not have a copy. Making regular backups is recommended.

9. Your rights and choices

Because I do not collect or hold your personal information, there is nothing for me to access, correct or delete on my side. For data collected by Google when you choose to watch an ad, your rights are described in Google's privacy policy, and you can contact Google directly. If you live somewhere with privacy rights, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California, you can still email me with a question or request.

10. Changes to this policy

If I change how the app handles data, I will update this page and the "Last updated" date, and mention significant changes in the app's release notes.
